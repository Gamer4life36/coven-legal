Coven — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026 · Developer: Gamer4life

Coven is a free dating and social app for the alternative community (goth, punk, metal, scene, and related subcultures). This policy explains what information Coven collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Coven has no ads, does not sell your data, and offers no paid features.

Who can use Coven

Coven is strictly for adults 18 years of age or older. You must confirm your age during sign-up. Accounts belonging to anyone under 18 will be removed.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your email address and a password (for accounts created with email), or an anonymous guest identifier. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text.
  • Profile information — the display name, age, pronouns, subgenres, favorite bands, interests, and short bio you choose to add, plus profile photos you upload.
  • Approximate location — if you grant permission, we use your device's coarse location to show approximate distance to other people ("N miles away"). We do not collect precise GPS location or track your location in the background.
  • Messages and shared photos — the messages and images you send to your matches, so they can be delivered and displayed.
  • Device push token — a notification identifier so we can send you alerts about new messages and matches.

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile.
  • To show you other users and let you match and chat with them.
  • To calculate approximate distance between users.
  • To deliver push notifications for new messages and matches.
  • To keep the community safe (handling blocks and reports).

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

What other users can see

Your profile (display name, age, pronouns, photos, subgenres, bands, interests, bio) and your approximate distance are visible to other Coven users as part of the matchmaking service. Messages and photos you send are visible to the match you send them to. Do not share information you would not want another user to see.

How your information is stored and shared

Coven's data is hosted by Supabase (authentication, database, and file storage) and push notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data on our behalf. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, and access is restricted by row-level security so users can only read data they are permitted to see.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: go to the You tab and tap Delete account. This immediately removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages from the app, and you disappear from other users.

For safety, security, and legal purposes, we retain a limited private copy of your profile and messages in a restricted internal log for up to 90 days after deletion — for example, to investigate abuse reports or comply with legal obligations. This copy is not visible to other users, is accessible only to the developer, and is permanently purged after that period. You may also request deletion using the form linked below.

Safety

You can block or report any user from their profile. Blocked users are removed from your discovery feed in both directions. Reports are reviewed and may result in content or account removal.

Children

Coven is not directed to and may not be used by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact & data-deletion requests

To delete your account, use the in-app Delete account button, or submit a request through our account deletion request page. For any privacy question, you can contact the developer through the app's Google Play Store listing page.
