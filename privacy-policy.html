Coven — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026 · Developer: Gamer4life

Coven is a free dating and social app for the alternative community (goth, punk, metal, scene, and related subcultures). This policy explains what information Coven collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Coven has no ads, does not sell your data, and offers no paid features.

Contact: merryjane1988@yahoo.com

Who can use Coven

Coven is strictly for adults 18 years of age or older. You must confirm your age during sign-up. Accounts belonging to anyone under 18 will be removed.

Information we collect

To run the service and keep it safe, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address and a password (for accounts created with email), or an anonymous guest identifier. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text.
  • Profile information — the display name, age, subgenres, favorite bands, interests, "vibe" songs, and the personal details you choose to add (such as star sign, stone, tarot card, moon phase, love language, and your green/red flag), plus profile photos you upload. You control what is shown publicly.
  • Photos and GIFs — profile photos, images and GIFs you send in chat, and images you add to your private vault.
  • Messages — the messages and images you send to your matches, so they can be delivered and displayed.
  • Approximate location — if you grant permission, we use your device's coarse location to show approximate distance to other people ("N miles away"). We do not collect precise GPS location or track your location in the background. You can deny location permission; distance simply won't be shown.
  • Device and connection identifiers — a device identifier (Android SSAID) and your IP address. We use these only for safety and fraud/abuse prevention (see below).
  • Device push token — a notification identifier so we can send you alerts about new messages and matches.
  • Reports you submit about other users, and technical logs needed to operate the app.

Why we collect it

  • To provide the service — create your profile, find matches, and deliver messages.
  • To keep Coven safe — detect and stop abuse, harassment, and prohibited content, and to enforce bans.
  • To prevent banned users from returning — we may block an email address, device identifier, and/or IP address associated with a banned account.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

Human moderation — please read

Coven is human-moderated for safety. Content you upload or send (including profile photos, chat messages and images, and vault images) and your safety signals (device identifier, IP address, approximate location) may be reviewed by our moderation team to detect predators and content that violates our rules. Reported accounts are reviewed with their relevant context. We keep this data only as long as needed for moderation and legal compliance.

If we discover content that is illegal — in particular child sexual abuse material — we will preserve it as required and report it to the appropriate authorities (in the United States, the NCMEC CyberTipline), and remove the offending account.

What other users can see

Your profile (display name, age, photos, subgenres, bands, interests, and the personal details you choose to show) and your approximate distance are visible to other Coven users as part of the matchmaking service. Messages and photos you send are visible to the match you send them to. Private vault images are shown to a match only while you have granted them access, and only for as long as you allow.

How we store and share it

  • Your data is stored with our cloud provider (Supabase), acting as our processor, and backed up to secured storage controlled by the developer.
  • Private vault images are stored in a private, access-controlled bucket.
  • Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google).
  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • We may disclose data where required by law or to protect users' safety.

Your choices and rights

  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from You → Delete account, or by using our account deletion request page. This removes your live data; limited records may be retained where required for safety or legal reasons (for example ban signals or sealed evidence).
  • Location — controlled by your device permission.
  • Notifications — controlled by your device permission.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK, or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. On deletion we remove your live data. Ban signals (email, device identifier, IP address) and any sealed evidence of illegal activity are retained as needed to keep banned users out and to meet legal obligations.

Children

Coven is 18+ only. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18. If we learn an account belongs to a minor, we remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
