Delete your Coven account & data

Coven · Developer: Gamer4life

You can permanently delete your Coven account and all associated data at any time.

Fastest way — delete in the app

  1. Open Coven and sign in.
  2. Go to the You tab.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm Delete forever.

This immediately and permanently removes your profile, photos, matches, and messages. It cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login (email, if any)
  • Your profile, bio, and all uploaded photos
  • Your likes, matches, and all messages and shared photos
  • Your notification/device token

Your data is removed from the app immediately and you disappear from other users. For safety, security, and legal purposes, a limited private copy of your profile and messages is retained in a restricted internal log (visible only to the developer) for up to 90 days, then permanently purged.

Can't access the app?

If you can no longer open the app, you can request deletion of your account and data by contacting the developer through the Coven listing page on the Google Play Store (use the "Contact developer" option). Please include the email address associated with your account so we can locate it. Requests are handled within 30 days.
